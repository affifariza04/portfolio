Dovetail: repositioning a research tool for the agent era

Dovetail reframes itself from a research repository into a “customer context layer” for AI agents, and leans on bold type and proof to carry it.

The job of the page

  • 01Keep research teams while winning new budget from AI initiatives
  • 02Prove the product is enterprise-safe and loved
  • 03Make an abstract layer feel concrete

What works

01Repositioned on the AI narrative

Sales calls, tickets and interviews become “context for agents”, each linked to evidence and revenue impact. The existing strength (evidence) becomes the answer to AI's weakness (trust).

02Instant trust in the corner

G2, Capterra and TrustRadius ratings sit top right, in the eye path, before any scroll.

03Display type with a wink

“Feed the facts machine” in huge type with a 3D fax machine between the words breaks enterprise seriousness and makes the abstract idea physical.

04A heavyweight logo wall

Mercedes-Benz, KPMG, Notion, Salesforce, Dyson, Shopify and Wise close the hero, answering “who else uses this?” for enterprise buyers.

dovetail.com · further down

Friction & opportunities

!The value proposition is a paragraph

The first message is one long sentence in small type on black; the big, memorable line appears only further down. Scanners read the logos, not the promise.

!Low-contrast supporting text

Grey text on near-black is elegant but harder to read, especially on laptops outdoors.

What I'd test next

ExperimentVariantSuccess metric
Swap the hierarchyUse “Feed the facts machine” as the H1 and the long sentence as subcopyDemo requests; scroll past the hero
Raise contrastLift body text contrast to WCAG AA on the heroReadability score; CTA click-through on mobile

Takeaway for my own work

A repositioning works best when the old strength becomes the answer to the new market's fear. Say it big, then explain.
